Who Am I?
I am a Software Engineer with over 7 years of experience building systems that solve real-life problems — now expanding into Automation Engineering to broaden how I design intelligent, cross-domain solutions. What Do I Know?
I have delivered across multiple engineering leadership roles — from driving system architecture as a technical lead, to supporting a team of 15+ Software Engineers and UI/UX Engineers as an engineering manager, to shaping the technical vision at Mechanico, a startup preparing for funding with a lean and high-impact engineering team. I’ve built and shipped systems using TypeScript, ReactJS, NextJS, NodeJS, Python, Flask, Django, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, GraphQL, WebSockets, and more. My ability to adapt across problem domains now extends into automation systems and intelligent real-time applications, bringing the same rigor I apply in software design.

Working with distributed teams across time zones has sharpened my ability to bring structure, clarity, and forward momentum to complex projects.

In the pipeline is my PhD in Computer Science, where I’m focused on building explainable and secure AI systems that operate in real time to power the next generation of automation and human-computer collaboration.

When I’m not writing code or working on research, I’m behind a camera capturing life’s rhythm. And once upon a time, I was a pro DJ, curating energy at events and shows.
